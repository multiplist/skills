# Multiplist primitives

Use this map when a distinction changes tool choice or interpretation. It describes relationships, not a fixed tool catalog or promise of access.

| Primitive | Meaning | Avoid this mistaken inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Saved material: a conversation, document, note, or generated brief. | A brief being returned as a Source is not a naming defect. |
| Brief | An Archivist research result with a lifecycle and a persisted Source. | Job/brief IDs and Source IDs are not interchangeable unless the response establishes their mapping. |
| Seed | Permanent, source-bound extracted knowledge: verbatim bites whose wording and provenance must be preserved. | A returned excerpt or abstract is not automatically a verified raw-source quotation. |
| Decision category | A category of extracted meaning. | Classification alone does not prove that a proposal was adopted. Check speaker, date, wording and later changes. |
| Citation | A reference to supporting evidence, often displayed as a numbered link. | The number is not a Source ID, seed ID or count of contributing Sources. Resolve the actual target and supporting passage. |
| Card | An ephemeral, action-oriented object in a Studio deck, organized in a lane and carrying its own identity. | Putting seed content on a card does not make the seed ephemeral or make its ID a Card ID. |
| Author annotation | A source author's explicit mark. | Account ownership is not proof of authorship; a caller label is not authentication. |
| Marginalia | A later reader's commentary, correction, tension or evolution mark. | It does not rewrite the original speaker's text or prove that an extractor adopted it. |
| Container, studio, lane and pin | Organization for work around vault material; a pin links material into that work. | A container does not thereby own or relocate the original Source. |
| Packaged skill | Guidance for an assistant workflow. | It is distinct from internal extraction, evaluation and production recipes. |

A card may carry a copy of seed content or reference a seed. Moving, completing or removing that workspace card must not be interpreted as changing the permanent seed or its Source. Select the primitive by purpose: preserve extracted knowledge as seeds; coordinate temporary work with cards.

Counts describe the response or operation that supplied them. A generated brief's own seedCount is not its number of research citations; excluding seeds from a Source response can produce a zero view count. Inspect available accounting fields and primitive kinds before describing a count as a vault total or number of extracted seeds. A Source with zero extracted seeds may still contain readable raw text. Authored marks do not establish that extraction occurred or that research used their content.

For exact passages, inspect returned stored text. Prefer document format with content explicitly requested when supported; the JSON view can omit the body while returning metadata. Use JSON when structured mark identities or other metadata are needed. Preserve raw wording and whitespace when quoting; keep any display limits distinct from storage truncation.

For business synthesis, distinguish:

- what a source says;
- whether and by whom a choice was explicitly adopted;
- whether a capability, price or buyer claim has been independently validated.

A historical proposal, founder preference or generated assertion is not sufficient evidence of current functionality, customer demand or a commercial commitment.
